What you'll actually do

You'll own one project end to end for twelve weeks. Not a slice of someone else's work — a whole thing, scoped against a target you have to move, built on real data, and shipped.

You'll work through the same three steps a paying client gets: scope against a measurable target before writing any model code, prototype on real data with the evaluation harness delivered alongside it, then deploy and hand over with documentation someone else can pick up. That sequence is the actual skill. Most graduates have never been made to do it.

Projects come from our own research and open-source work — vision pipelines, agent orchestration, evaluation tooling, edge deployment. Everything you build is published under your own name.

You own the work

Your project ships as a public repository with you credited as the author. It is yours to show, discuss and build on afterwards. We are not going to bury it.

Direct access, no layers

QVisionX is one person and a fleet of AI agents. You will not be managed through three levels of hierarchy — you work with the founder directly, weekly.

You will be paid

A monthly stipend for the full twelve weeks. We think unpaid technical work dressed up as opportunity is a bad deal, so we do not offer one.

A written reference that means something

At the end you get a reference describing what you actually built and what you were good at — specific enough to be useful, because it is based on real work.

Who this is for

We care about what you can build, not where you studied. There is no institution filter and no minimum CGPA.

Final-year students and recent graduates

Graduating this year or within the last two. Any discipline — the strongest applicants often did not study computer science.

You have built something

A project, a tool, a notebook, a bot, something half-finished. It does not have to be impressive or polished. It has to be yours, and you have to be able to explain it.

Comfortable with Python

You can read and write it without a tutorial open. You do not need machine-learning experience — several projects need good engineering more than they need models.

Available for the full twelve weeks

Roughly full-time. This does not work as a few hours around a full course load, and we would rather say so now than three weeks in.

How selection works

No aptitude tests, no puzzle rounds, no unpaid take-home that takes a weekend.

01

You apply

The form below. The one question that matters is the last one — it is read properly, and it is what most decisions turn on.

02

We read everything and reply

Every applicant gets an answer, including a no. Being ignored after applying is the worst part of graduate hiring and it is entirely avoidable.

03

A conversation, not an interrogation

Shortlisted applicants have a 45-minute call about something you have built. No whiteboard algorithms, no trick questions.

04

Five offers

With the stipend, the dates and the project you would own, in writing, before you commit to anything.

What this isn't

Worth being straight about

  • This is not a guaranteed job. QVisionX is a one-person company; we cannot promise a role at the end, and anyone who promises you one at this stage is guessing.
  • You will not work on client projects. We are early and do not have client work to hand out — you will build our own research and open-source projects, which is why we can publish everything.
  • It is remote and largely self-directed. You get weekly time with the founder and help when you are stuck, but nobody is watching your calendar.
  • Twelve weeks is short. You will ship one thing properly rather than sample five things shallowly.
