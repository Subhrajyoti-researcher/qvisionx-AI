The studio

One founder,
a fleet of agents.

QVisionX is deliberately small. Priyanka Mandal architects every system and owns the outcome; a fleet of AI agents handles research, code generation, testing and deployment around the clock. For larger builds, specialist engineers join for the duration.

The result is a studio that moves at the speed of a very small team and holds itself to the standards of a much larger one. Nothing ships without an evaluation set, and nothing is handed over without documentation someone else can pick up.

Method

Measured,
not asserted.

Scope against a measurable target. Before any model work, we agree the number the system has to move — recall, cycle time, cost per task. Without one, "better" is a matter of opinion.

Prototype on your data. A narrow, evaluable build against real inputs and real failure cases, with the evaluation harness delivered alongside it.

Deploy and hand over. Production deployment on your infrastructure, with monitoring, documentation, and a team that can run it without us.
